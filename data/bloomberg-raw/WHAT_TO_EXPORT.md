# Bloomberg exports checklist (save as Excel/CSV where possible)

- [ ] NVDA price history: daily, 5+ years (HP / GP)
- [ ] Financials: income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, 5 yrs + quarterly (FA)
- [ ] Revenue by segment: Data Center, Gaming, Pro Visualization, Automotive (FA / segment data)
- [ ] Margins: gross, operating, net (FA)
- [ ] Consensus estimates + forward projections: revenue, EPS, next 2–3 yrs (EEO / EE)
- [ ] Analyst ratings + price targets (ANR)
- [ ] Valuation multiples vs peers: P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/S (RV / COMP), with AMD, INTC, AVGO, TSM, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN
- [ ] Ownership / institutional holders (HDS), optional
- [ ] ESG: Bloomberg ESG scores, emissions Scope 1/2/3, energy, water, governance (ESG tab) → save into `data/esg/`
- [ ] DCF / any of our own valuation models

Name files clearly, e.g. `nvda_price_history_2026-11.xlsx`.
