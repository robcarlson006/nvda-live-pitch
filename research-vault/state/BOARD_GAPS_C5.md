# C5: ten difficult board questions and readiness

Compared the current vault Q&A with the 35 existing questions in the project Q&A bank. “Answered” means an evidence-linked answer was added in this pass, not that every underlying uncertainty is resolved. Existing charts are referenced in the Q&A.

1. **Is using AI for this presentation environmentally wasteful?** Answered in Q0012; the actual presentation footprint remains unmeasured. V0052 gives wider data-centre demand context, not a presentation-specific footprint.
2. **Does low operational emissions intensity prove NVIDIA is cleaner than its chip manufacturer?** Answered in Q0013 with V0036 and V0053; differing business models, years and boundaries limit comparability.
3. **Why did the social disclosure score fall, and do the scores imply worse workforce practices?** Answered in Q0014 with V0024; the cause and scoring methodology remain unverified.
4. **How vulnerable is NVIDIA to losing a major customer?** Answered in Q0015 with V0017; direct customers are not necessarily end users.
5. **How defensible is the valuation if the discount rate is disputed?** Answered in Q0016 with V0013; both figures are model scenarios.
6. **Can the board rely on management and governance during the reported leadership and government inquiry events?** Research needed: verify the precise timeline, current status and primary filings before repeating the project's account.
7. **What happens if Taiwan or TSMC manufacturing or advanced packaging is disrupted, and can NVIDIA recover?** Research needed: supply concentration, alternatives, timing and scenario implications.
8. **How much revenue and growth are exposed to China export restrictions, and what restrictions apply now?** Research needed: current rules, effective dates, exposure and company disclosures.
9. **Is AI demand circular because NVIDIA invests in customers that buy its hardware?** Research needed: sourced transaction terms, materiality and accounting; distinguish disclosed commitments from realised revenue.
10. **How does NVIDIA identify and remedy forced-labour risks in its global supply chain?** Research needed: current supplier audits, corrective action, coverage and independent evidence, plus applicable UK disclosure.
