# C10: ten difficult board questions and readiness

Compared all 26 vault questions existing at the start of this pass with the 35 questions in the project Q&A bank. The ranking favours material investment and ESG risks that remain only partially answered; a safe admission of missing evidence is not a fully supported answer.

1. **Are NVIDIA’s indirect emissions actually falling as its business grows?** Addressed by Q0027 and chart V0053. The primary report and changing measurement boundaries still need verification.
2. **Does NVIDIA’s Ecolab partnership prove data centres save water?** Addressed cautiously by Q0028; a quantified, independently measured comparison and suitable chart remain missing.
3. **What would a Taiwan or TSMC disruption do to supply, and how quickly could alternatives replace it?** Research needed on fabrication, advanced packaging, alternatives and time to recover; the existing bank answer needs corroboration.
4. **How much revenue is exposed to current China export controls, and how do the rules affect future sales?** Research needed on enacted requirements, effective dates, reported exposure and financial impact.
5. **Is NVIDIA financing customers to buy its own chips, creating circular demand?** Research needed on actual investments, commitments, counterparties, accounting and materiality.
6. **How does NVIDIA detect and remedy forced labour throughout its supplier chain?** Research needed on audit coverage, findings, remediation, independent verification and UK disclosures.
7. **How much water do NVIDIA and its key suppliers withdraw and consume in stressed locations?** Research needed on current primary reporting, definitions, site exposure and comparable series.
8. **Does NVIDIA have credible, independently validated climate targets covering the most material value-chain emissions?** Research needed on target status, baseline, coverage, supplier commitments and absolute emissions.
9. **Will custom chips and competing accelerators undermine NVIDIA’s margins and software advantage?** Research needed on independent workload benchmarks, comparable economics, switching costs and disclosed margins.
10. **Are data-centre customers earning enough on AI to sustain purchases, and what happens to NVIDIA’s valuation if spending pauses?** Research needed on disclosed customer returns and capex, demand uncertainty and explicitly modelled downside scenarios.

## Integrity and conflict review

No confirmed conflicting values for the same metric and period were found, so no canonical fact was fabricated. Apparent differences include distinct Scope categories, different customer groups or denominators, different facilities, and different projections or scenarios.
