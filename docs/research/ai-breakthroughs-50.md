# 50 things AI has done: pick 5 for the "AI is moving every industry" slide

Compiled 24 Nov 2026 for the pitch. Already in the deck, so left out here: the 2024 Chemistry Nobel (protein AI), the lung-disease drug reaching trials, and Swedish breast-cancer screening.

**Status column.** ✓ = checked against a source on 24 Nov 2026 (link given). **Check** = well-documented, but not re-checked today: find the primary source before it goes on a slide. Every figure that goes on screen still has to go into a curated dataset (`research_ai_breakthroughs.json`, `verified` true/false), never typed into slide text.

**Watch for:** several Google DeepMind results (GraphCast, AlphaEvolve, AlphaFold) ran on Google's own TPU chips, a rival to NVIDIA. They still prove AI demand, but a sharp board member may point it out. NVIDIA-powered examples (for example John Deere's See & Spray) make the cleaner story.
