# Jensen Huang: resignation and reinstatement (research, 23 Nov 2026)

Researched on the day of the news, two days before the pitch (25 Nov). Everything here is from reporting and company filings published up to about 12:30 ET on 23 Nov. **This is moving fast. Re-check everything on the morning of the pitch.**

**How this was gathered:** Obscura 0.2.7 fetched the pages below. Obscura's search route (DuckDuckGo) hit a CAPTCHA, which I did not try to get past. Headlines came from Google News RSS through Obscura, and articles were then read directly. A few headlines came from the built-in web search tool, and I mark where a claim rests only on that.

**Handling note:** several allegations concern a living person's private life and an unproven espionage suspicion. They are written below as what outlets and officials reported, not as established fact. The primary sources are NVIDIA's own press releases and SEC 8-K filings.

## 1. What the company itself has said (primary sources)

- **12 Nov (8-K, press release):** Huang resigned as president, CEO and director, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO while keeping the CFO role. Harvey C. Jones was named the board's first-ever Chair. The board began a search for a permanent CEO. Third-quarter results were to be reported on 17 Nov as scheduled.
- **23 Nov (8-K, press release, "NVIDIA Board Reinstates Jensen Huang..."):**
  - The board voted unanimously to reinstate him as president, CEO and director, effective immediately, after "a series of discussions and meetings" that left it satisfied with his "candor and ability to lead".
  - The board "received input from senior levels of the United States government" and says it is confident his reinstatement "poses no national security risk".
  - The board-formed committee of independent directors, advised by independent counsel, is still investigating the reports that he "has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort". It will "proceed to conclusion". Huang says he supports it and will cooperate.
  - A new **National Security and Export Control Committee** (independent directors only) will oversee trade compliance and national security risk.
  - NVIDIA is searching for a new **Chief Compliance Officer**, reporting to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee.
  - NVIDIA "will continue to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries."

## 3. Why the board reversed (what is reported, and what isn't known)

- **Reported factors:** pressure from Trump, who said he spoke to the board; a petition (Yahoo says more than 100,000 signatures; Wikipedia says more than 178,000 in four days, so the counts differ); public support from tech leaders; the market reaction; and the board's own statement that its meetings with Huang satisfied it on candor. The company's statement does not mention the petition or Trump by name.
- **The company's formal reason:** candor and ability to lead, plus government input that there is no national-security risk. The statement does not say what the government input was.
- **Trump's stated position:** the government has "no reason to believe" his return poses a risk, and Attorney General Blanche says the US "does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct" (per NPR).
- **Not known:** what changed between 17 Nov ("does not intend to reinstate") and 23 Nov beyond the above; what the board learned in its meetings; and what the investigation will conclude.
- **Surprise factor:** Yahoo Finance says Polymarket gave him about 3% odds of being reinstated that month minutes before the news, then about 99%.

## 4. Market impact (numbers differ by source)

- **Reinstatement day:** CNN says the stock rose more than 30%, Nasdaq nearly 7%, S&P 500 5%, Dow 3.1%. Yahoo's ticker showed +32.5%. An Investing.com headline said 35%. Use "about 30%+" until you have an official close.
- **The drop:** Yahoo says about $1.2 trillion of market value was lost from the 11 Nov close to Friday 20 Nov, including the stock's worst week ever, and that the rebound recovered nearly $1 trillion. Wikipedia says about $2.7 trillion down from the 6 Oct peak by 20 Nov. These are different baselines, not a contradiction.
- **Fundamentals unchanged:** per the search summary of NVIDIA's own release (I could not fetch the release itself), Q3 FY2027 revenue was $115.8 billion, up 103% year on year, with Data Center at $107.6 billion, a Q4 guide of $130 billion plus or minus 3% that assumes no China data-centre compute revenue, and supply commitments of $390 billion. **Verify these on investor.nvidia.com before using any of them.** They conflict with some figures in the existing script, which cites last year's numbers.

## 5. Open risks and unresolved items

- The independent investigation (Kim/Wachtell) continues, so the outcome is unknown.
- **Senate Banking Committee testimony:** Huang is to testify publicly "next week" (NPR; several headlines).
- Congressional criticism of the reinstatement (a Reuters headline says US lawmakers criticised it; I could not read the article).
- DOJ criminal probe of Megaspeed; the NYT reports Megaspeed bought more than $4 billion of NVIDIA chips (via Wikipedia).
- **NVIDIA's own conduct is in question:** NYT reporting (via Wikipedia) says Megaspeed's Malaysian subsidiary kept receiving NVIDIA technology for eight months after the no-sell listing, including more than $800 million of export-controlled technology mostly through third-party server makers; NVIDIA says Commerce permitted some repair-part shipments. Treasury Secretary Bessent said the real question is whether NVIDIA knew its customer and whether anyone should be held accountable.
- Alice Huang's alleged intelligence ties are denied by China, and Megaspeed says her role ended in 2025. The US government's own officials disagree on how strong the evidence is.

## 6. Governance angle (matters most for an ESG board)

Sources: Al Jazeera/Reuters explainer, Fortune (two pieces: "stay, with less" and the Sonnenfeld Caremark column; I read the parts available), City AM, CNN's board list.

- **Structure:** 11-member board, all but Huang independent. He owns about 3.5–3.6% of the stock, with no majority control and a single share class. Under the bylaws he can be removed with or without cause by majority vote.
- **Criticism:** long-serving directors with decades of ties to Huang (Fortune's "captive board" framing); no board chair until 12 Nov; a flat organisation with dozens of direct reports and no named successor; "next to no succession planning" (City AM opinion); no director with a national security background (Semafor, per a search summary).
- **Fortune/Sonnenfeld's argument:** the problem wasn't the share structure but the board's deference; shareholders could bring oversight ("Caremark") claims in Delaware by asking what the board knew and what it did. Per The Information (via Wikipedia), the board was reluctant to act and the general counsel had to urge it to open the investigation.
- **Reforms announced on 23 Nov:** national-security committee, new chief compliance officer, investigation continuing. Whether this answers the governance critique is an opinion. Commentators haven't settled it.

## 7. What this means for the pitch

Rob's call, not mine. These are flags from the research.

1. **The "G" in ESG is now the board's first question.** A sceptical ESG board will raise it. The existing script's Beat 14 ("NVIDIA isn't one person... Glassdoor ranked NVIDIA best place to work") and any "Jensen's leadership" framing are now sensitive, and the Glassdoor claim was already flagged as unverified.
2. **Key-person risk is the investment question.** The events showed the market treats Huang as worth hundreds of billions of dollars in market value. That is both a bull point (the market's confidence in him) and a bear point (dependence on one person, thin succession).
3. **Don't litigate the private-life allegations on stage.** The factual, safe frame is governance and process: what the board did, what it changed, what's still unresolved.
4. **Counterpoint for "Wall Street hated it":** that beat is about the CUDA bet. It still works, but the board may now hear any founder-hero narrative through this news.
5. **The live ticker and any slide with a price will look dramatic.** The stock moved about 30% in one day. Prepare the narrative before it appears on screen.
6. **Figures in the script are likely stale.** The Q3 FY27 release (17 Nov) may make several script numbers out of date. Check `data/clean` against the release.
